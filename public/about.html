Gabriel Marinov (call Marinov) is an computer science student who loves Android development, embedded systems, computer networks and open-source development, he studies at Centro Universitário FEI (São Paulo, Brazil).

He developed:
  • MarinovWRT: a fork of OpenWRT for routers and network devices;
  • OpenFEI: an Android/web app alternative to FEI bad application;
  • MGS (Marinov Game System): a complete guide to turning a Linux mini PC into a living-room console experience — waking from suspend with a Bluetooth controller and automatically switching TV input and launching Steam Big Picture automatically.
Even more...

He also contributes for Android Custom ROM community, he is the mantainer of a Lineage OS 22.2 GSI and is working on kernel upstream for Banana-Pi M5 and LG G Pad 7 (LG V400).

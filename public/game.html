MarinovTetris
An opensource Tetris game, enjoy!
How to play:
"W" or "Up" key or tap on screen: rotate piece;
"S" or "Down" key or press screen or press/swipe down screen: speed-up drop;
"A" or "Left" key or swipe left: move piece to left;
"D" or "Right" key or swipe right: move piece to right;
Survive as long as you can...

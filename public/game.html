MarinovTetris [BETA]
An opensource Tetris game, enjoy!
How to play:
"W" or "Up" key: rotate piece
"S" or "Down" key: speed-up drop
"A" or "Left" key: move piece to left
"D" or "Right" key: move piece to right
Survive as long as you can...
